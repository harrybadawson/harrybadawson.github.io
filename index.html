About me

[Open with one sentence on what drew you to the field — the question you'd still want answered if funding were no object. Two or three sentences is plenty.]

My work sits between observation and theory. On the observational side I build and analyse samples of hot subluminous stars from wide-field surveys, using spectroscopy to measure atmospheric parameters and radial velocities. On the theoretical side I compare those samples against binary population synthesis models to test which formation channels can reproduce what we actually see.
