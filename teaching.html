Teaching and supervision

2021 to present

Ondřejov observational techniques workshop

Project lead and joint organiser of an annual two week research workshop at Ondřejov Observatory in the Czech Republic, teaching master's students to observe with the 2 m Perek telescope. This covers planning the night, photometric and spectroscopic observations, and then reduction and analysis.

2019 to 2020

OST lab course supervisor and telescope operator

University of Potsdam. I ran the 50 cm OST observatory and taught the 2019 and 2020 master's cohorts to plan, optimise and reduce their own observations.
